## Update contract

The supervisor updates this board for every captain-relevant phase change: a new decision request, review-ready result, material blocker or failure, hold or resume, and final outcome.
Keep each entry concise, preserve any captain-authored response, and remove obsolete entries after acknowledgement.
Routine progress and automatic retries update neither this board nor captain-facing chat.
Captain-facing chat is reserved for a direct question, an explicitly requested status, or urgent destructive, irreversible, security-sensitive, credential, blocker, or failure escalation.
